Privacy Policy

For ReezoAI Assist by Cognito Innovations
Effective date: January 1st, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Cognito Innovations (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates ReezoAI Assist, a Shopify application that helps customers discover and browse products through guided category suggestions on merchant storefronts (https://cognitoinnovations.com).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to merchants and store visitors when using ReezoAI Assist.

This policy applies to the ReezoAI Assist Shopify app and related services. It does not cover the privacy practices of Shopify or of individual merchant storefronts, which have their own policies.

2. Who this policy covers

  • Merchants — Shopify store owners and staff who install and configure ReezoAI Assist.
  • Store visitors / shoppers — people who use the product discovery widget on a merchant's online store.

3. What type of personal information do we collect?

You can learn about ReezoAI Assist without providing personal information. To install the app, sync products, or use certain storefront features, we may collect the information described below.

Merchant and store information

This may include your Shopify shop domain and shop identifiers, authentication and session data needed for the embedded Shopify Admin experience, product catalog data synced from your Shopify store (including product titles, descriptions, pricing, categories, and inventory status), app settings such as widget preferences, and billing or subscription contact details if you request paid or enterprise plans.

Information accessed through Shopify

Depending on the permissions granted at install, ReezoAI Assist may access Shopify data such as products, orders, and metaobjects as needed to operate within your store. Access is limited to the scopes approved during installation.

Storefront widget and shopper information

When shoppers use the widget, we may process category selections and product browsing interactions; a randomly generated guest identifier stored in the browser; basic usage events (for example, widget opened, categories browsed); and browsing preferences stored locally in the shopper's browser for a seamless experience on that device. We do not collect personal identifiable information from shoppers in the current version.

Note: Email collection and advanced features are planned for future versions and are not currently active.

Technical and support information

We may collect standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, and request paths), plus error and diagnostic information needed to keep the service reliable and secure.

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain ReezoAI Assist for merchants and their shoppers
  • Sync product catalog data and organize products by category for browsing
  • Display relevant product suggestions based on category selections
  • Authenticate merchants via Shopify and secure admin sessions
  • Store and sync product catalog data and related settings
  • Understand basic product usage and improve performance and reliability
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about the app
  • Comply with legal obligations, including Shopify's privacy compliance webhooks
  • Detect, prevent, and address security or abuse issues

We do not sell personal information. Your product data is securely stored and never shared with third parties except as described in this policy.

5. How information is shared

We may share information only as needed to run the service, including with:

  • Shopify — to authenticate, install, and operate within the Shopify platform
  • Infrastructure providers — including Supabase (database hosting) and Vercel (application hosting) where product data and app settings are securely stored and processed
  • Service providers — vendors who help us operate the app under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations
  • Legal / compliance — when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect rights and safety

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Merchants remain responsible for how they use ReezoAI Assist on their storefronts and for providing their own customer-facing privacy disclosures where required.

6. Cookies and local storage

ReezoAI Assist uses browser storage technologies (such as local storage and sessionStorage) to keep merchant sessions working, remember guest identifiers, and store recent browsing preferences on the shopper's device.

Specifically, we use:

  • Session cookies — for merchant authentication via Shopify OAuth
  • Local storage — to remember shopper's browsing preferences and category selections
  • Session storage — for temporary session data that expires when the browser is closed

Shoppers can clear this data through their browser settings. Clearing browser data may reset the browsing preferences on that device. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics cookies.

7. Data retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide ReezoAI Assist services:

  • Merchant product catalog data — retained while the app is installed. Automatically deleted within 48 hours after app uninstall or when a shop redaction request is received
  • Shopify session data — retained as required for authentication. Deleted when a shop requests redaction after uninstall or within 48 hours of uninstall
  • Shopper browsing preferences — stored locally in the browser only, not on our servers. Remains until cleared by the shopper
  • Server logs — retained for 30 days maximum for security and debugging purposes, then automatically deleted

Upon app uninstall, we comply with Shopify's mandatory webhooks to delete all merchant and shop data.

8. Data deletion and Shopify compliance

ReezoAI Assist supports Shopify’s mandatory compliance webhooks, including:

  • customers/data_request — requests for customer data associated with a shop
  • customers/redact — requests to delete customer data associated with a shop
  • shop/redact — requests to delete shop data after uninstall (including session cleanup)

Merchants or shoppers who need help with access or deletion requests can contact us using the details below. Merchants should also use Shopify’s tools where applicable.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, including secure transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and verification of Shopify webhook authenticity. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

ReezoAI Assist is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.
