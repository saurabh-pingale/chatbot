Cognito Assistant

Privacy Policy

For Cognito Assistant by Cognito Innovations
Effective date: July 1st, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Cognito Innovations (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates Cognito Assistant, a Shopify application that helps merchants answer shopper questions using a storefront chatbot powered by merchant-managed FAQs (https://cognitoinnovations.com).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to merchants and store visitors when using Cognito Assistant.

This policy applies to the Cognito Assistant Shopify app and related services. It does not cover the privacy practices of Shopify or of individual merchant storefronts, which have their own policies.

2. Who this policy covers

  • Merchants — Shopify store owners and staff who install and configure Cognito Assistant.
  • Store visitors / shoppers — people who use the chatbot widget on a merchant’s online store.

3. What type of personal information do we collect?

You can learn about Cognito Assistant without providing personal information. To install the app, configure FAQs, or use certain storefront features, we may collect the information described below.

Merchant and store information

This may include your Shopify shop domain and shop identifiers, authentication and session data needed for the embedded Shopify Admin experience, FAQ content you create (questions and answers, including bulk CSV uploads), app settings such as fallback messages and chatbot preferences, and billing or subscription contact details if you request paid or enterprise plans.

Information accessed through Shopify

Depending on the permissions granted at install, Cognito Assistant may access Shopify data such as products, orders, and metaobjects as needed to operate within your store. Access is limited to the scopes approved during installation.

Storefront chatbot and shopper information

When shoppers use the widget, we may process chat messages entered to match questions to your FAQs; a randomly generated guest identifier stored in the browser; optional email address and one-time verification codes if you enable an email gate; approximate location and IP-related information that may be collected when a shopper opens the chatbot; basic usage events (for example, chatbot opened); and conversation history stored locally in the shopper’s browser so the chat can resume on that device.

Technical and support information

We may collect standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, timestamps, and request paths), plus error and diagnostic information needed to keep the service reliable and secure.

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Cognito Assistant for merchants and their shoppers
  • Match shopper questions to merchant FAQs and display relevant answers
  • Authenticate merchants via Shopify and secure admin sessions
  • Store and sync FAQ content and related settings
  • Understand basic product usage and improve performance and reliability
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about the app
  • Comply with legal obligations, including Shopify’s privacy compliance webhooks
  • Detect, prevent, and address security or abuse issues

We do not sell personal information. We do not use shopper chat content to train third-party foundation models for unrelated purposes.

5. How information is shared

We may share information only as needed to run the service, including with:

  • Shopify — to authenticate, install, and operate within the Shopify platform
  • Infrastructure providers — such as hosting, databases, and related cloud services used to store and process app data
  • Service providers — vendors who help us operate the app under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations
  • Legal / compliance — when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, or to protect rights and safety

Merchants remain responsible for how they use Cognito Assistant on their storefronts and for providing their own customer-facing privacy disclosures where required.

6. Cookies and local storage

Cognito Assistant may use browser storage technologies (such as cookies, local storage, and IndexedDB) to keep merchant or shopper sessions working, remember guest identifiers, store recent chat history on the shopper’s device, and support optional email verification flows when enabled by the merchant.

Shoppers can clear this data through their browser settings. Clearing browser data may reset the chat history on that device.

7. Data retention

  • Merchant FAQ content and settings are retained while the app is installed and as needed to provide the service
  • Shopify session data is retained as required for authentication and is deleted when a shop requests redaction after uninstall (subject to Shopify’s timelines)
  • Shopper conversation history stored in the browser remains until cleared by the shopper or by using the chatbot’s clear-conversation control
  • Logs and analytics data are retained only as long as reasonably needed for security, operations, and improvement

8. Data deletion and Shopify compliance

Cognito Assistant supports Shopify’s mandatory compliance webhooks, including:

  • customers/data_request — requests for customer data associated with a shop
  • customers/redact — requests to delete customer data associated with a shop
  • shop/redact — requests to delete shop data after uninstall (including session cleanup)

Merchants or shoppers who need help with access or deletion requests can contact us using the details below. Merchants should also use Shopify’s tools where applicable.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, including secure transmission (HTTPS), access controls, and verification of Shopify webhook authenticity. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s privacy

Cognito Assistant is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. International transfers

Information may be processed and stored in countries where we or our service providers operate. Those countries may have data-protection laws different from those in your jurisdiction. By using Cognito Assistant, you understand that information may be transferred to such locations as needed to provide the service.

12. Merchant responsibilities

If you are a merchant using Cognito Assistant, you are responsible for:

  • Providing your own privacy notice to store visitors as required by law
  • Ensuring FAQ content and chatbot settings do not unlawfully collect or disclose personal data
  • Using optional features (such as email collection) in a lawful manner, including obtaining any required consent
  • Responding to customer privacy requests related to your store where you are the controller

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of Cognito Assistant after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
